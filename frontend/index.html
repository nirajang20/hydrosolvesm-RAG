Evaluator information

Privacy and research use

Your name, email, and affiliation will be collected for research administration. Your evaluation scores will be analyzed and reported using a participant ID rather than your name or email. Identifying information will not be included in published results or publicly shared datasets. Access will be restricted to the authorized research team. Your survey progress is stored in Cloudflare's database.

Provide this notice only if it matches your actual research practices and any required ethics/IRB approval. Initials are not inherently anonymous.

All 30 responses can be completed across multiple visits on this device.
